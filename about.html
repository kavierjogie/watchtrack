About WatchTrack

A watchlist and episode tracker for anime, TV shows and movies

WatchTrack is a web app for keeping track of what you watch. It keeps anime, TV series and movies in one library, remembers the exact next episode for every show, and sorts everything into lists like Watching and Plan to Watch. There's no sign-up: your library is saved in your own browser.

It's built for people who juggle several shows at once, come back to a series after months away, or watch both anime and Western TV and don't want two separate trackers.

Track anime, TV series and movies

Add a title by searching for it, and WatchTrack fills in the poster and episode counts. Anime comes from Kitsu, TV series from TVmaze, and movies plus extra TV results from TMDB. You can also enter anything by hand and upload your own poster, which works offline.

Organise your watchlist

  • Five lists: Watching, Plan to Watch, On Hold, Completed and Dropped. A title is always in exactly one.
  • One library for every format: filter by anime, TV or movie when you want to see just one kind.
  • Ratings and notes: rate titles 1–10 and keep a private note on each.
  • Discover: recommendations based on the genres and ratings already in your library.
  • Share a read-only list: pick titles and send a link. The list is stored inside the link itself, and your friend doesn't need an account.

Keep track of your viewing progress

  • Always know the next episode. Each show in progress shows what to watch next, with a one-tap Watched button and Undo.
  • Overall or per-season numbering. Long-running anime are often counted as one running number; most TV is season + episode. Pick per title.
  • Seasons handled carefully. When a season ends, WatchTrack moves you to the next one only if it knows it exists. It never assumes.
  • Skips and specials. Mark episode ranges to skip (recaps, filler), and log specials or OVAs without moving your place.
  • Upcoming releases. Episode counts refresh automatically, and you can get alerts for new episodes and seasons while the app is open.

How WatchTrack works

  1. Open WatchTrack in any modern browser. Nothing to install or sign up for; you can add it to your home screen as an app if you like.
  2. Add a show, anime or movie: search for it, or type it in yourself.
  3. Set where you are (the last episode you watched, or the next one to watch).
  4. Tap Watched after each episode. Your Home screen keeps everything in progress in one place.
  5. Export a backup now and then from Settings, so your library is safe and can move to another device.

Privacy and data storage

Your library (titles, progress, history, notes, ratings and uploaded posters) is stored in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) on the device you're using. There's no WatchTrack account, and the library is never uploaded to a WatchTrack server.

  • What does leave your device: when you search, the search text is sent to Kitsu, TVmaze or TMDB (TMDB requests pass through WatchTrack's own server so its API key stays private). Refreshing episode counts and recommendations sends those services the ids of the shows involved and genre names, never your progress, notes or ratings. Like any website, these services see your IP address.
  • Visit counting: the site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookie-free. It records the page address (with title ids, searches and share-link contents removed), referrer, browser, device type and country.
  • Your responsibility: clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your library, and other browsers and devices have their own separate, empty copy. Nothing syncs automatically. Use Export backup in Settings to keep a copy and to move between devices.

Frequently asked questions

Is WatchTrack free?

Yes. There's no paid plan, no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the site and start adding titles. Because there's no account, there's also no password to lose and no profile for anyone else to see.

Does my watchlist sync between my phone and computer?

No. Each browser keeps its own library. To move it, export a backup file on one device and import it on the other; the import shows duplicates and lets you merge or replace.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once it has loaded. Marking episodes, editing lists and adding titles by hand all work offline. Searching for new titles needs a connection.

Can I import my list from another tracker?

Not yet. Import only reads WatchTrack's own backup files.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The interface is built for phones first. In your browser's menu choose Add to Home Screen (or Install) to open it like an app.
