Privacy Policy

Your watchlist is yours

Last updated 29 October 2026

WatchTrack is a free watchlist and episode tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies, and it never sells or shares your data. This page explains exactly what is stored, where, and what leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

Your library (titles, progress, watch history, notes, ratings, uploaded posters and preferences) is stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on the device you use. It is not uploaded to a WatchTrack server. The developer cannot see it.

Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it, unless Google Drive sync is on (below).

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings:

  • WatchTrack asks Google for one permission, drive.appdata. It lets the app create and read its own file in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It does not give access to any of your other files, folders, documents or photos.
  • That file contains your WatchTrack library (the same data listed above). It's used only to keep your library safe and the same across your devices.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored in your browser. WatchTrack's server is used only to exchange that token with Google (Google requires a secret key that can't be put in a web page). It does not store, log or read your token or your library.
  • The developer has no access to your Google account, your Drive or the synced file.
  • Disconnect in Settings stops syncing and revokes WatchTrack's access. To delete the synced file too, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps, find WatchTrack and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

WatchTrack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

What leaves your device

  • Title search and details: search text, and the ids of shows in your library, are sent to Kitsu, AniList, TVmaze and TMDB to find titles, posters, episode counts and recommendations. TMDB requests pass through WatchTrack's server so its API key stays private. Your progress, notes and ratings are never sent.
  • Visit counting: Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies. It receives the page address (with title ids, searches and link contents removed), referrer, browser, device type and country.
  • Hosting: the site and its small server functions run on Vercel. Like any website, Vercel and the services above see your IP address.
  • Links you create: share links and transfer links carry their contents inside the link itself (after the #, which browsers don't send to servers). Anyone you give a link to can read it. Transfer links include notes, so send them only to yourself.
  • Notifications: optional, shown by your own browser. Nothing goes to a push server.

Your choices

  • See everything WatchTrack stores about you in the app itself: it's your library.
  • Delete it any time with Settings → Clear all data, and remove the Google Drive copy as described above.
  • Move it to another device with Google Drive sync or a transfer link.

Children

WatchTrack doesn't collect personal information, and it isn't directed at children under 13.
