Privacy Policy

Your watchlist is yours

Last updated 30 October 2026

WatchTrack is a free watchlist and episode tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies, and it never sells or shares your data. This page explains exactly what is stored, where, and what leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

Your library (titles, progress, watch history, notes, ratings, uploaded posters and preferences) is stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on the device you use. It is not uploaded to a WatchTrack server, except for the titles you choose to share (see Links you create). The developer cannot see your library.

Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it, unless Google Drive sync is on (below).

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings:

  • WatchTrack asks Google for one permission, drive.appdata. It lets the app create, read and delete its own files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It does not give access to any of your other files, folders, documents or photos.
  • Those files contain your WatchTrack library (the same data listed above): the current copy, plus up to 4 weekly safety copies you can restore from in Settings. They're used only to keep your library safe and the same across your devices, and they count towards your Drive storage.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored in your browser. WatchTrack's server is used only to exchange that token with Google (Google requires a secret key that can't be put in a web page). It does not store, log or read your token or your library.
  • The developer has no access to your Google account, your Drive or these files.
  • Disconnect in Settings stops syncing and revokes WatchTrack's access. To delete the synced files too, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps, find WatchTrack and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

WatchTrack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

What leaves your device

  • Title search and details: search text, and the ids of shows in your library, are sent to Kitsu, AniList, TVmaze and TMDB to find titles, posters, episode counts and recommendations. TMDB requests pass through WatchTrack's server so its API key stays private, and Kitsu poster images pass through it so they can be saved for offline use (it stores nothing). Your progress, notes and ratings are never sent.
  • Visit counting: Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies. It receives the page address (with title ids, searches and link contents removed), referrer, browser, device type and country.
  • Hosting: the site and its small server functions run on Vercel. Like any website, Vercel and the services above see your IP address.
  • Links you create: when you press Create link, only the titles you picked are stored for 90 days on Upstash, through WatchTrack's server, and then deleted automatically. That covers each title's name, type, status, progress, year, rating, poster address and catalogue id, never your notes, history or settings. Your IP address is kept only in hashed form, for up to an hour, to limit how many links can be created. Anyone you give a link to can read it. To have a link removed sooner, email the address below with the link. If a short link can't be created, and for older share links, the list is carried inside the link itself (after the #, which browsers don't send to servers). Older transfer links work the same way and include notes.
  • Backup files: a backup is saved to your device, or wherever you choose to share it. It contains your whole library, including notes, so keep it somewhere private.
  • Notifications: optional, shown by your own browser. Nothing goes to a push server.

Your choices

  • See everything WatchTrack stores about you in the app itself: it's your library.
  • Delete it any time with Settings → Clear all data, and remove the Google Drive copy as described above.
  • Move it to another device with Google Drive sync or a backup file.

Children

WatchTrack doesn't collect personal information, and it isn't directed at children under 13.
