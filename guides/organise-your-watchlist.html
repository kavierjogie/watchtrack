How to organise your movie, TV and anime watchlist

Updated 29 October 2026

Most watchlists fail the same way: everything goes in, nothing comes out, and after a few months the list is too long to help you pick anything. A small amount of structure fixes that.

Keep everything in one list

Separate lists for movies, TV and anime sound tidy, but they split one question ("what should I watch tonight?") across three places. Keep a single library and filter by type when you want to. It also means a movie that belongs to a series sits next to the series itself.

Use five statuses, and give each one a clear meaning

  • Watching: things you're actively watching this week or month. Keep it short; this is your "what's next" list.
  • Plan to Watch: things you genuinely intend to start. Not everything anyone has ever recommended.
  • On Hold: paused on purpose, such as waiting for the next season or saving it for later. You still plan to finish.
  • Completed: finished. Rate it now while you remember how it felt.
  • Dropped: you've decided not to finish. Moving a show here is a decision, not a failure, and it clears space in Watching.

The difference between On Hold and Dropped matters most. If a show has sat in On Hold for months with no reason to return, it's dropped. Be honest and the rest of the list stays trustworthy.

Add a reason when you add a title

"Why did I add this?" is the question that kills plan-to-watch lists. A one-line note, like who recommended it or what it's similar to, makes the list far easier to pick from later.

Review your plan-to-watch list occasionally

Every month or two, skim Plan to Watch and remove anything you no longer care about. A short list you trust beats a long one you ignore.

Rate what you finish

Ratings aren't only for other people. Over time they show you which genres and formats you actually enjoy, which makes choosing what to add next much easier.

Share the list instead of retyping it

When a friend asks what to watch, send them a list rather than a screenshot. They can see the titles, your progress and your ratings in one place.
